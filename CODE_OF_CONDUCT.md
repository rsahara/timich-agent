# Code of Conduct

This project is a practical space for building and maintaining Timich Agent.
Everyone who participates is expected to keep collaboration respectful,
focused, and safe for people with different backgrounds and levels of
experience.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind, patient, and specific when giving feedback.
- Assume good intent, while still making room to correct mistakes quickly.
- Keep technical disagreement about the work, not the person doing it.
- Respect maintainers' time by keeping issues and pull requests focused.
- Avoid sharing private data, secrets, or sensitive operational details.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, or sustained disruption.
- Demeaning language related to identity, background, experience, or ability.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Publishing private communications, personal information, credentials, or
  operational secrets without explicit permission.
- Bad-faith reports, repeated off-topic demands, or behavior that makes
  collaboration unsafe.

## Reporting and Enforcement

If you see a conduct problem, contact a maintainer privately when a private
channel is available. If no private channel is available, open a minimal public
issue asking for a maintainer contact path without including sensitive details.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, limit access,
or take other moderation action when needed to protect the project community.
